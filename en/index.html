Why calorie calculators fail

Formulas estimate.
Your body does its own maths.

Formula said

1,648

versus

Actually measured

816

A professional sports assessment measured a basal metabolic rate of roughly 816 kcal at 63 kg body weight — more than 800 kcal below what standard formulas predict. Base your target on a number like that and you never actually eat in a surplus, then wonder why nothing happens.

That is one real measurement, not an average — but precisely why GAINZ does not derive your target from a formula. It calculates it from your own data and raises it in small steps.

How it works

Four problems. Four answers.

Your target is measured, not guessed

GAINZ works out your real maintenance intake from what you log and your smoothed weight trend — then nudges the target upward instead of overwhelming you with a wishful number.

  • Maintenance = average intake minus your weight trend
  • +100–150 kcal every 1–2 weeks, as long as the trend holds
  • Target rate 0.25–0.5 % of body weight per week
  • Protein 1.6–2.2 g/kg — stays constant even when calories are low

The plan makes the volume doable

The blocker is rarely willpower — it's volume. You're full long before the target is met. GAINZ spreads the calories across six slots: proper meals and deliberately calorie-dense snacks.

  • Weekly plan from 500+ meals, respecting your blocklist and gluten-free setting
  • Cooking mode: quick ≤ 15 min, meal prep, or mixed
  • Shopping list in real pack sizes — with leftovers accounted for
  • Log by search, barcode scan or a one-tap snack

Train wherever you happen to be

No gym, no time, no energy — the three most common reasons people stop. So GAINZ only asks: where are you, how long have you got, how strong do you feel? — and hands you a finished workout.

  • Living room, balcony, on the road, or a gym
  • 5, 10, 20 or 40 minutes — “tired” is a valid answer too
  • Automatic progression: more reps, harder variants, planned deload
  • Strength and cardio steered separately, with recovery protected

Stick with it, without the guilt

At 0.25 % gain per week the scale stays quiet for weeks. Watch only the scale and you quit. So GAINZ rewards what you can actually control.

  • Weekly review with a process score instead of daily noise
  • Forgiving streak with a joker week — one missed day doesn't count
  • If-then plans that remind you in your own words
  • Widget with a one-tap snack, and at most one reminder a day
  • 500+ meals plus optional AI recipes to fight boredom

Built on evidence

No bro science.

The rules behind the plan and the training come from sports science and behavioural research — not from gut feeling.

Building muscle

Low loads taken close to failure drive hypertrophy about as well as heavy loads (Schoenfeld) — which is why home training works. Target volume is 10–20 hard sets per muscle per week, with protein at 1.6–2.2 g/kg.

Sticking with it

If-then plans are the strongest known adherence lever. Habits take around 66 days on average (Lally) — which is why the streak forgives a slip instead of punishing it.

Questions

Briefly answered.

Is the app available in English?

Not yet. Version 1.0 ships in German; the app is already built for localisation, so English is the next step rather than a rewrite. Drop us a line and we'll tell you when it lands.

Is GAINZ just another calorie counter?

No — it runs the other way round. GAINZ is built to hit an achievable surplus rather than police a deficit. Your calorie target is calibrated from your own data and raised step by step, and it comes with training planning and a behaviour engine aimed at excuses.

Do I need a gym?

No. GAINZ is designed for home training: body weight, resistance bands, a pull-up bar, adjustable dumbbells, or simply a chair and a wall. If you do have gym access, the “gym” location unlocks heavy compound lifts as well.

What if I don't know my basal metabolic rate?

That's fine — the measured value is optional and only ever used as a starting estimate. GAINZ deliberately starts low and works out your real requirement over the first 10–14 days from your logging and weight trend.

Do I need an AI key?

No. The weekly plan, training, coach guidance and progress all work without AI. If you want, you can add your own API key so text reads more naturally and extra recipes can be generated. Every assessment and calculation still happens in the app itself.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The meal catalogue, exercise library and all calculations live on the device. Only the food search (Open Food Facts) and the optional AI features need a connection — lose it, and local search takes over.

Is this medical advice?

No. GAINZ is a wellness app and is no substitute for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you are underweight, or before changing your diet, please consult a doctor.

Frequently asked questions

Is the app available in English?

Not yet. Version 1.0 ships with a German interface. The app is already prepared for localisation, so English is a translation step rather than a rewrite. Email us and we'll let you know when it's available.

How is my calorie target calculated?

GAINZ doesn't guess, it calibrates: from what you log and your smoothed weight trend it derives your real maintenance intake, then raises the target gradually. A measured basal metabolic rate is only ever used as a starting estimate. GAINZ is a wellness app and is no substitute for medical advice.

How do I enable or disable Apple Health?

Under Profile → Apple Health. Access is optional and can be revoked at any time there or in iOS Settings.

What does the AI coach do with my data?

The AI features are optional and off by default. They use your own API key, stored only in your device's keychain. Before the first transfer the app asks for explicit consent; only summarised training and recipe data is sent — never weight or health values. Details in the privacy policy.

How do I delete all my data?

Profile → Legal & data → “Delete all data”. This irreversibly removes all local data, the iCloud backup and the stored AI key. The app then starts fresh, as on first launch.

Does GAINZ work without iCloud or without an internet connection?

Yes. The app works entirely locally. The iCloud backup is optional, and if the connection drops the online food search falls back to local results.

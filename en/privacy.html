2. This website

2.1 Hosting and server logs

This website is served as a static site via GitHub Pages. The provider is GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (a Microsoft Corporation company).

When you open the site, technically necessary data is transmitted to the provider's servers and processed in log files, in particular:

  • the truncated or full IP address of the requesting device
  • date and time of access
  • name and URL of the file requested, and the volume of data transferred
  • browser and operating system used, and where applicable the referring page

Purpose: delivering the page, ensuring stability and security, and preventing abuse. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in operating the website securely and reliably. We do not combine this data with other sources and have no access to the log files ourselves.

Third-country transfer: data may be transferred to the USA. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, so the transfer can rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision under Art. 45 GDPR; the standard contractual clauses under Art. 46 GDPR apply in addition. See GitHub's privacy statement.

2.2 No cookies, no tracking, no external content

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking services and embeds no external resources. Fonts, images, the video and the stylesheet are all served from this website itself — so no connection is made to providers such as Google Fonts, CDNs or social networks. Consent under § 25 TDDDG is therefore not required and there is no cookie banner.

The promotional video is served from this website (no YouTube, no Vimeo) and is only loaded once you start it.

3. Contacting us

If you email us — for example via the “Notify me” button or the support page — we process the data you send (email address, name if given, and the content of your message) in order to answer your enquiry.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the enquiry relates to preparing or performing a contract, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding to enquiries). If you ask to be notified when the app is released, storing your address for that purpose is based on Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent), which you may withdraw at any time.

We delete your enquiry once it has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply — notification requests at the latest after the single notification, or immediately on withdrawal. Email is sent via the mailbox provider of the address given; transmission over the internet may be unencrypted.

4. The GAINZ app

The app requires no user account and no registration. Everything you record — meals, calories, weight, body data, workouts, sets, plans, recipes and reminders — is stored locally on your iPhone in an app database. We, as the provider, do not receive this data and have no access to it. Data leaves your device only in the clearly defined cases below.

4.1 Apple Health (HealthKit) — optional

If you enable it in the app, GAINZ reads your weight from Apple Health and writes new weigh-ins and completed workouts back to it. This is health data within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR. Processing takes place exclusively on your device; this data is not transmitted to us or to third parties and is not used for advertising or analytics.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR — your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time with future effect, in the app or in iOS Settings.

4.2 iCloud backup (CloudKit) — optional

If your device is signed in to iCloud, GAINZ can back your app data up to your private iCloud database so it stays in sync and protected across your devices. Processing happens within your Apple account by Apple Inc. or Apple Distribution International Ltd.; only you have access, not us. Apple's privacy policy applies. Without iCloud the app continues to work entirely locally.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent by enabling the feature), otherwise Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR to provide the feature.

4.3 Food data — Open Food Facts

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search term or barcode is sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve product and nutrition data. No personal data and no identifier of yours is transmitted; the app only sends a technical name and version as its user agent. The operator is the non-profit organisation Open Food Facts (France, EU).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the search function you invoked).

4.4 Camera and barcode scanning

The app accesses the camera for barcode scanning. Image processing happens entirely on the device; no images are stored or transmitted — only the recognised digit sequence is used for the product lookup.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (camera permission).

4.5 Exercise library — wger

When the exercise library is loaded once, exercise data is retrieved from wger.de (provider in the EU). No personal data is transmitted.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

4.6 AI features — optional, with explicit consent

GAINZ can phrase coach text more naturally and generate additional recipes. These features are disabled by default and require you to store your own API key for an AI provider. The key is stored encrypted in your device's iOS keychain only and is never transmitted to us.

The app asks for your explicit consent before the first transfer. Only then are summarised training and recipe data transmitted — for example the number of sets, slot calorie targets, blocked ingredients and the recipe wish you typed. Weight and health values are not transmitted.

The default provider is Mistral AI (Mistral AI SAS, Paris, France — processing in the EU), privacy notice. Depending on the app variant, Anthropic (Anthropic PBC, USA) may be used instead; in that case a third-country transfer takes place based on your explicit consent under Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR and the provider's standard contractual clauses — privacy notice.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent). You can switch the feature off and remove the key in the app at any time, which ends any further transfer.

4.7 Notifications

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications directly on the device. There is no push server and no transmission to us. You can disable notifications at any time in the app and in iOS Settings.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent by enabling notifications).

4.8 App Store

The app is downloaded via the Apple App Store. Apple processes data (e.g. Apple ID, download time) under its own responsibility. From Apple we receive only aggregated, non-personal statistics. Apple's privacy policy applies.

7. Retention and deletion

  • App data: kept until you delete it. You can delete all data in the app at any time: Profile → Legal & data → “Delete all data”. This removes the local data, the backup in your private iCloud and the stored AI key. Individual entries can be removed directly in the respective lists.
  • Website server logs: deleted by the host according to its own schedule, usually after a short period; we have no access to them.
  • Email enquiries: deleted once dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply (e.g. § 147 AO, § 257 HGB).
  • Notification requests: deleted after the single notification, or immediately on withdrawal.

8. Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data at any time:

Access (Art. 15 GDPR)
Confirmation of whether and which data we process about you, together with further information.
Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
Correction of inaccurate data or completion of incomplete data.
Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
Deletion of your data, provided no retention obligations prevent it.
Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
Restriction instead of erasure, for example while accuracy is being verified.
Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
Receipt of the data you provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
Objection (Art. 21 GDPR)
Objection to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Withdrawal of consent at any time with future effect — for Apple Health, iCloud, notifications or the AI features, directly in the app or in iOS Settings.

9. Automated decisions, necessity, changes

No automated decision-making

There is no automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. The app's recommendations (calorie target, workout suggestions) are calculated solely on your device, have no legal effect and can be overridden by you at any time.

Whether provision is required

You are not obliged to provide any personal data. No registration is needed to use the app. All optional features (Apple Health, iCloud, notifications, AI) can be left disabled — the app remains fully usable.

Changes to this policy

We update this privacy policy when the app, this website or the legal situation changes. The current version is always available at this address; the date above shows its status.

Not a medical service

GAINZ is a wellness and fitness app and is no substitute for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
